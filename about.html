About Our Organization

Officially registered under the Societies Registration Act, serving with complete governance and transparency.

Our Objective & Purpose

Creation Social Justice Welfare Society was registered on 28th September 2018 (Registration Number: S-1300/2018) with a primary mission: to foster social justice, protect civil rights, and uplift marginalized communities through active welfare projects.

Operating from North East Delhi, our initiatives range from providing educational support to underprivileged children, organizing health camps, extending humanitarian aid during crises, and offering community arbitration to ensure harmony and justice.

Our Core Values

Integrity & Transparency

Direct deployment of resources to genuine grassroots causes.

Equal Opportunity

Ensuring education, basic dignity, and rights for all sections of society.
